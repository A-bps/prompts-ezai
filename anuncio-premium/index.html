Prompt de anúncio premium sem cara de IA

O prompt que eu usei no Gemini para transformar uma arte com cara de IA em foto de anúncio profissional.

  1. Toque em Copiar prompt.
  2. Abra o Gemini (ou o ChatGPT) e anexe a foto do seu produto.
  3. Cole o prompt e troque os campos entre colchetes pelo texto do seu anúncio.
Prompt
AÇÃO:
Create a premium commercial advertising photograph for the dietary supplement shown in the uploaded reference image.

REFERENCE PRODUCT — ABSOLUTE PRIORITY:
Use the uploaded product image as the exact visual reference for the product.

The supplement bottle and its box must remain visually faithful to the original product, including:
- bottle shape
- bottle proportions
- cap shape
- cap color
- packaging
- box dimensions
- label design
- logo
- brand name
- typography
- colors
- icons
- printed information
- product name
- positioning of all printed elements

Do NOT redesign the packaging.
Do NOT invent or rewrite text.
Do NOT generate a new logo.
Do NOT modify the brand identity.
Do NOT change the product formulation visually.
Do NOT alter the number of capsules shown if visible.
Do NOT add labels, badges, seals or certifications that are not present in the reference.

OBJECTIVE:
Transform the basic product photograph into a high-end professional advertising photograph suitable for a premium supplement brand.

The result must look like a professionally produced commercial campaign photographed in a high-end advertising studio.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
Minimalist, sophisticated, premium, powerful and modern.

The composition should have strong visual hierarchy.

The supplement must be the clear hero of the image.

Create depth through:
- foreground/background separation
- realistic perspective
- controlled depth of field
- subtle atmospheric depth
- realistic shadows
- dimensional lighting
- natural reflections
- layered composition

The product must have strong visual presence without making the image visually crowded.

COMPOSITION:
Use a clean and intentional commercial composition.

Place the product slightly off-center or in a visually balanced position.

Create generous negative space around the product.

Do not fill the entire image with text, icons or decorative elements.

Avoid visual clutter.

The advertisement should communicate sophistication through composition, lighting and product presentation rather than through excessive graphic elements.

BACKGROUND:
Create a premium, elegant background inspired by the product's existing visual identity.

Use subtle dark and sophisticated tones that harmonize with the original packaging.

The background should have:
- smooth gradients
- subtle depth
- controlled highlights
- soft atmospheric texture
- professional studio ambience

Avoid busy backgrounds.

Avoid excessive patterns.

Avoid unnecessary objects.

Avoid unrealistic environments.

LIGHTING:
Use cinematic commercial-product lighting.

Create:
- large soft key light
- controlled fill light
- subtle rim lighting
- realistic highlights
- soft contact shadows
- subtle reflections
- dimensional contrast

The lighting should reveal the physical characteristics of the packaging and make the product feel premium.

Do not overexpose the label.

Do not create excessive gloss.

Do not use fake glowing effects.

Do not make the product look artificially rendered.

MATERIAL REALISM:
Preserve realistic materials:
- plastic
- cardboard
- printed label
- metallic cap
- glossy surfaces

Show subtle realistic reflections and micro-textures.

The product must look physically present in the scene.

CAMERA:
Professional commercial product photography.

Full-frame camera look.

Premium studio lens.

Natural perspective.

Excellent depth of field.

Sharp focus on the product.

Background softly separated from the subject.

No wide-angle distortion.

No warped packaging.

No deformation of the bottle or box.

COLOR:
Maintain the product's original colors accurately.

Use sophisticated cinematic color grading around the product without altering the product itself.

Colors should feel rich, controlled and premium.

Avoid oversaturation.

Avoid excessive HDR.

Avoid artificial neon colors.

TEXT:
Keep the amount of advertising text MINIMAL.

The image should not become a poster overloaded with information.

Use at most:
1 main headline
1 short supporting phrase
1 CTA

If text is added, keep it extremely clean, short and highly legible.

Suggested structure:

HEADLINE:
"[INSIRA A CHAMADA PRINCIPAL]"

SUPPORTING TEXT:
"[INSIRA UMA FRASE CURTA]"

CTA:
"[INSIRA O CTA]"

Typography:
- modern
- premium
- bold
- clean
- highly readable
- excellent hierarchy
- generous spacing

Do not cover the product.

Do not place text over important packaging details.

Do not generate illegible typography.

IMPORTANT:
If the image-generation model cannot accurately render the requested text, leave a clean designated area for typography instead of generating distorted or incorrect text.

ADVERTISING STYLE:
Luxury commercial photography
Premium supplement advertising
High-end product campaign
Editorial advertising photography
Cinema-quality lighting
Photorealistic
Extremely detailed
Professional art direction
Premium brand aesthetic
Clean visual hierarchy
Sophisticated composition
Realistic product photography

NEGATIVE REQUIREMENTS:
NO clutter
NO excessive text
NO random objects
NO floating capsules unless they exist in the reference
NO fruits or ingredients unless specifically requested
NO leaves or botanical elements unless specifically requested
NO laboratory equipment
NO medical symbols
NO fake certifications
NO fake awards
NO additional logos
NO invented claims
NO invented statistics
NO exaggerated health claims
NO before-and-after imagery
NO unrealistic effects
NO distorted packaging
NO incorrect brand name
NO altered product design
NO duplicated products
NO deformed bottle
NO deformed box
NO AI artifacts
NO cartoon appearance
NO 3D-render appearance

FINAL RESULT:
The final image must look like a real premium advertising photograph of the exact product shown in the reference image.

It should feel:
clean,
expensive,
credible,
modern,
professional,
visually striking,
and commercially ready.

The design philosophy is:

LESS INFORMATION.
MORE IMPACT.

Let the product, lighting, depth, composition and premium visual treatment do most of the communication.
